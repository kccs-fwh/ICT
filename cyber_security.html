Pretexting Scenario

Pretexting involves creating a fabricated storyline (pretext) where an attacker poses as an authorized authority figure (e.g. IT Helpdesk manager calling during an urgent system maintenance window) to manipulate victims into divulging sensitive 2FA codes or access tokens.

[SIMULATED AUDIO CALL SCRIPT]:

"Hello, this is David from Corporate SecOps. We noticed suspicious login activity from your account. I need you to confirm your 6-digit MFA push code immediately to prevent lockout..."

DEFENSE RULE: Always verify identity via official internal callback numbers before sharing access parameters!

Baiting Tactics

Baiting entices victims with physical or digital rewards—such as leaving an infected USB flash drive labeled "Executive Salaries 2026" in a company parking lot, or offering free media downloads loaded with malware.

Catfishing & Persona Fraud

Catfishing creates fictional online personas on social media or messaging networks to build deceptive emotional relationships with victims for blackmail, financial extortion, or identity theft.

Quid Pro Quo (This for That)

Quid Pro Quo promises a service or benefit in exchange for sensitive information—for example, pretending to offer free software tech support if the employee disables their local antivirus software.
