Train Your AI & Match the Emojis!

Teach a machine learning model to recognize your facial expressions, then race the clock to match each emoji on your webcam!

Create 3 Classes

Open Google Teachable Machine (Image Project) and name 3 classes: Happy, Sad, and Silly (Optional: add a 4th Neutral class for resting faces!)

2

Train & Export Link

Record webcam samples for each face and click Train Model. Then click Export Model → Upload my model and copy your shareable link.

Paste & Start Playing!

Paste your Teachable Machine link below, pick your game timer, and hold each matching face in front of your webcam to score points!
